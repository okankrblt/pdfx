2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Collect Automatically

Anonymous User Identifier

When you first launch the App, we automatically create an anonymous account on your behalf using Supabase, our backend provider. This account is identified only by a randomly generated UUID (a string like a3f9c1d2-...). This ID contains no personal information and is not linked to your name, email address, or any other identifying information unless you choose to link an account.

This ID is used solely to maintain your session within the App.

Device Storage (Local Only — Never Uploaded)

The following data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party:

  • A list of up to 10 recently opened PDF files (file name and path only)
  • Your theme preference (light, dark, or auto)
  • PDF files you create, edit, convert, or merge using the App

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We strictly do not collect or monitor:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you voluntarily link an account)
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, calendar, or camera
  • Any content within your PDF files
  • Any behavioral analytics or usage tracking
  • Device identifiers such as IMEI or MAC address (this is outside our app's code)

2.3 Information Collected by Third Parties

The App integrates third-party services that have their own privacy practices. We do not control the data these services collect.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Free users see non-personalized interstitial advertisements rated PG with child-directed settings enabled.

Supabase (Backend Infrastructure)

Powers our database system and processes anonymous user UUIDs securely.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous account
  • Serve non-personalized advertisements
  • Maintain your theme preference and recent file list locally on your device

We absolutely do not use your information for behavioral advertising or data renting.

4. Your Files and Documents

All PDF files you open, edit, convert, or create within the App remain on your device. File contents are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you use the share function, your device's native sharing system is used — we do not intercept or receive a copy of the shared file.

Temporary Document Clean-Up

Temporary files created during editing or conversion operations (e.g., converted_<timestamp>.pdf) are stored in your device's application storage directory. These files are not automatically deleted. You can remove them by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

5. Permissions We Request

Android

  • Read/Write External Storage (Android 12 and below): Required to open and save PDF files you select.
  • Internet Access: Required to initialize authentication and load advertisements.
  • Network State: Required to determine connectivity before network operations.

iOS

  • Photo Library (Read): Required only when you choose to import images for conversion to PDF. You are prompted before access is granted.
  • Photo Library (Write): Required only when you choose to save converted pages to your photo library. You are prompted before access is granted.

The App does not request permission to access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or calendar for any functional purpose.

7. Data Security

All communication between the App and our servers (Supabase) uses HTTPS encryption. Your anonymous session token is stored securely using SQLite-based local storage provided by Expo. We do not store payment card information — all payment processing is handled by Apple's App Store.

However, the App is a general-purpose productivity tool not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at artokn7@gmail.com and we will delete it.
