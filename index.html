Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 26, 2026 Developer: artokn

PDF X: Editor & Converter ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is developed and operated by artokn. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the App.

1. Introduction

We are dedicated to maintaining a high standard of document privacy. The App is engineered as a privacy-focused tool where your source files are processed client-side. We do not inspect your document contents, nor do we run background user tracking utilities.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Collect Automatically

Anonymous User Identifier

When you first launch the App, we automatically create an anonymous account on your behalf using Supabase, our backend provider. This account is identified only by a randomly generated UUID (a string like a3f9c1d2-...). This ID contains no personal information and is not linked to your name, email address, or any other identifying information unless you choose to link an account (see Section 2.3).

We store the following associated with your anonymous ID in our remote database:

  • Whether you have an active premium subscription
  • Your subscription expiration date (if applicable)

Device Storage (Local Only — Never Uploaded)

The following data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party:

  • A list of up to 10 recently opened PDF files (file name and path only)
  • Your theme preference (light, dark, or auto)
  • PDF files you create, edit, convert, or merge using the App

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We strictly do not collect or monitor:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you voluntarily link an account)
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Your contacts, calendar, or camera
  • Any content within your PDF files
  • Any behavioral analytics or usage tracking
  • Device identifiers such as IMEI or MAC address (this is outside our app's code)

2.3 Information You May Voluntarily Provide

If you choose to purchase a premium subscription, you may optionally link your Apple ID, Google account, or email address to your anonymous account. This is encouraged so your subscription persists if you reinstall the App. If you choose to link an account, we will store your email address or linked account identifier in our Supabase database.

2.4 Information Collected by Third Parties

The App integrates third-party services that have their own privacy practices. We do not control the data these services collect.

Google AdMob (Advertising)

Free users see non-personalized interstitial advertisements rated PG with child-directed settings enabled.

RevenueCat (Subscriptions - iOS)

Processes transaction details, billing events, and iOS subscription states securely.

Supabase (Backend Infrastructure)

Powers our database system and processes anonymous user UUIDs and subscription parameters securely.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above to:

  • Create and maintain your anonymous account
  • Track your premium subscription status so you receive the correct experience
  • Serve non-personalized advertisements to free users
  • Process in-app purchases and restore them on reinstall (if you link an account)
  • Maintain your theme preference and recent file list locally on your device

We absolutely do not use your information for behavioral advertising or data renting.

4. Your Files and Documents

All PDF files you open, edit, convert, or create within the App remain on your device. File contents are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you use the share function, your device's native sharing system is used — we do not intercept or receive a copy of the shared file.

Temporary Document Clean-Up

Temporary files created during editing or conversion operations (e.g., converted_<timestamp>.pdf) are stored in your device's application storage directory. These files are not automatically deleted. You can remove them by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

5. Permissions We Request

Android

  • Read/Write External Storage (Android 12 and below): Required to open and save PDF files you select.
  • Internet Access: Required to initialize authentication and load advertisements.
  • Network State: Required to determine connectivity before network operations.

iOS

  • Photo Library (Read): Required only when you choose to import images for conversion to PDF. You are prompted before access is granted.
  • Photo Library (Write): Required only when you choose to save converted pages to your photo library. You are prompted before access is granted.

The App does not request permission to access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or calendar for any functional purpose.

6. Data Retention Policy

Data Type Storage Location Retention Duration
Anonymous User ID & Subscription Status Supabase Cloud Servers Until you request account deletion
Recent Files History List Local Device Cache Until app data is cleared or uninstalled
Theme and Configuration Settings Local Device Cache Until app data is cleared or uninstalled
Temporary Converted/Edited PDFs Local Application Folder No automatic deletion; cleared on uninstall
Your Original PDF Documents Local Device Directories Never collected or stored on our side

7. Data Security

All communication between the App and our servers (Supabase) uses HTTPS encryption. Your anonymous session token is stored securely using SQLite-based local storage provided by Expo. We do not store payment card information — all payment processing is handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play Store systems.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is configured with child-safe advertising settings (child-directed treatment enabled in AdMob). However, the App is a general-purpose productivity tool not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at support@pdfx.app and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

All Users:

Request deletion of your anonymous account and associated data by contacting support@pdfx.app. Because your account is anonymous, we may ask you to provide your anonymous user ID (visible in app settings) or other verification.

European Union / EEA (GDPR):

You hold rights to access, rectification, erasure ("right to be forgotten"), restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to lodge complaints with a supervisory authority.

California (CCPA / CPRA):

You hold rights to know what personal info is collected, delete personal info, and opt-out of data sales (we do not sell your personal data).
