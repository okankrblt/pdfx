2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information We Collect Automatically

Anonymous User Identifier

When you first launch the App, we automatically create an anonymous account on your behalf using Supabase, our backend provider. This account is identified only by a randomly generated UUID (a string like a3f9c1d2-...). This ID contains no personal information and is not linked to your name, email address, or any other identifying information unless you choose to link an account (see Section 2.3).

This ID is used solely to maintain your session within the App.

Device Storage (Local Only — Never Uploaded)

The following data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third party:

  • A list of up to 10 recently opened PDF files (file name and path only)
  • Your theme preference (light, dark, or auto)
  • PDF files you create, edit, convert, or merge using the App

2.2 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you voluntarily link an account)

  • Device identifiers such as IMEI or MAC address (this is outside our app's code)

The App integrates third-party services that have their own privacy practices. We do not control the data these services collect. The App contains no advertising and no in-app purchases.

We use the information described above to:

4. Your Files and Documents

All PDF files you open, edit, convert, or create within the App remain on your device. File contents are never uploaded to our servers or any third party. When you use the share function, your device's native sharing system is used — we do not intercept or receive a copy of the shared file.

Temporary Files

Temporary files created during editing or conversion operations (e.g., converted_<timestamp>.pdf) are stored in your device's application storage directory. These files are not automatically deleted. You can remove them by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

5. Permissions We Request

Android

  • Read/Write External Storage (Android 12 and below): Required to open and save PDF files you select
  • Internet Access: Required to initialize authentication
  • Network State: Required to determine connectivity before network operations

iOS

  • Photo Library (Read): Required only when you choose to import images for conversion to PDF. You are prompted before access is granted.
  • Photo Library (Write): Required only when you choose to save converted pages to your photo library. You are prompted before access is granted.

The App does not request permission to access your microphone, camera, location, contacts, or calendar for any functional purpose. If such a permission appears on your device, it is a residual declaration from a third-party library dependency and is not exercised by this App.
